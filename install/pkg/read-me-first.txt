UA-101 Driver: read me first
============================

For the Edirol / Roland UA-101, on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later), macOS 14 Sonoma or
later.

1. Double-click "UA-101 Driver.pkg".

2. If macOS says it can't check the file and won't open it: this installer isn't signed through
   Apple's Developer ID program. Click Done. Then:
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
     - scroll down to Security,
     - next to '"UA-101 Driver.pkg" was blocked', click Open Anyway,
     - type your Mac password, and click Open Anyway once more.
   The installer opens. This is needed once for each downloaded copy.

3. Follow the installer. Quit apps that play sound first: at the end all sound and MIDI stop
   for a moment.

4. Open "UA-101 Control" from Applications. Its Help menu has the manual.

The same manual is beside this file: "UA-101 Control Manual.pdf". Its page "Install, update
and remove" says more.

To remove the driver later: in UA-101 Control, choose UA-101 Control > Uninstall UA-101...
Two choices:
  - Remove the Driver, Keep My Settings: your Driver Settings and the app's choices stay,
    for a later install.
  - Remove Everything: every UA-101 file and setting goes, in every account. A tick keeps
    your backup files and reports (Documents > UA-101 Backups, UA-101 Reports).
macOS asks for an administrator's password. If the app is already gone, type this in Terminal
(it asks the same choices):

    "/Library/Application Support/ua101-driver/uninstall.sh"

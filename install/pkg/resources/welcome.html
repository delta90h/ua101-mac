UA-101 Driver

This puts in a driver for the Edirol / Roland UA-101 USB audio interface, for Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

After the install:

  • Every app sees a sound device called UA-101, with 10 inputs and 10 outputs.
  • MIDI apps see the port UA-101 MIDI, and Audio MIDI Setup shows the device UA-101.
  • The app UA-101 Control is in your Applications folder: the mixer, the patch bay, backups and the unit's memories. Its Help menu has the full manual.

Before you go on: quit apps that play or record sound. At the end all sound and MIDI on this Mac stop for a moment, while Core Audio starts again. The unit can be on or off.

Nothing in macOS's security settings is changed, and System Integrity Protection stays on. Nothing is written to the unit's memory.

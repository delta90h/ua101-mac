UA-101 Driver 1.1.2.

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later), macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Tested on macOS 27.2 on an M1 MacBook Pro.

- No blue frame on a button as a window opens. With Keyboard navigation on (System Settings → Keyboard), macOS drew a blue frame around Driver Settings… as the mixer opened. The mixer, its windows, Patch Bay and Driver Settings now open with no button chosen; press Tab to choose one with the keyboard, as before.

Everything from 1.1.1 is in it too: a cleaner uninstall, windows that always fit the screen, Recall Memory's Continue coming on by itself, and the checksum below.

Install: double-click `UA-101 Driver.pkg` (your browser may save it as `UA-101.Driver.pkg`), over 1.1.1, 1.1 or 1.0, or on a clean Mac. It isn't signed through Apple's paid developer program, so the first time macOS won't open it: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (see the README).

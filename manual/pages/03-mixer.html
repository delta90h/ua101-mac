Use the mixer

The mixer sets what you hear in the monitors and headphones (the UA-101's direct monitor mix). It does not change what the Mac records.

The three groups

  • Input Monitor (INPUT 1 to 10, into the monitor mix): the sockets of the box. 1 and 2 are the MIC PRE sockets on the front, 3 to 8 the LINE sockets on the back, 9 and 10 the OPTICAL input.
  • Output Monitor (OUTPUT 1 to 10, from the computer): the sound the Mac plays to the box.
  • MONITOR OUT: the whole mix, as it goes to MONITOR OUT and PHONES. The mix is also sent to the Mac: a music app can record it as input 11 and 12 (→ IN 11, → IN 12).

MONITOR OUT has MUTE, a fader and LINK, but no SOLO and no pan.

The keyboard

The same are in the Mixer menu: Select Channel, Mute, Solo, Pan, Volume and Link. Their lists also name every channel, so you can reach any channel without selecting it first. The Mute menu is the MUTE button.

The buttons at the top

  • Driver Settings…: see Driver Settings.
  • Messages: opens the Messages window; the number is how many new messages wait. See The Messages window.
  • Patch Bay…: see Patch Bay.
  • Recall…, Save… and Reset…: the same as Recall Memory…, Save Memory… and Reset to Factory… in the Device menu.

The MIDI IN and MIDI OUT lights blink green while MIDI passes through the box's MIDI sockets.

If SOFT CTRL is out

The box ignores the mixer until its SOFT CTRL switch is pressed in: inputs and the computer's outputs pass as they are. Press SOFT CTRL in on the box; the note goes by itself. The patch bay works either way.

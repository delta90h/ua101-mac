Sound comes from the Mac's speakers, not the boxChoose UA-101 again in System Settings → Sound (or in the music app). This can happen after the box was switched off or unplugged for a while. See When the Mac sleeps, or the box goes away.
The music app shows it is playing, but there is no soundWas the box off or unplugged? Stop and start playing again, and check the app's output is UA-101. Check the box's volume knobs and the Patch Bay.
No MIDI in the music appCheck the cable: the keyboard's MIDI OUT goes into the box's MIDI IN. Play a key: the mixer's MIDI IN light should blink. If it does, choose UA-101 MIDI as the input in the app, or quit and open the app again. See MIDI.

Questions

Does UA-101 Control have to be open for sound or MIDI?
No. The driver works by itself. The app is only for the box's mixer, patch bay, memories and the driver's delay.
Does the box keep my settings when I switch it off?
Yes: the UA-101 saves the settings in use by itself (unless you paused it, see Auto-Save). The five memories are kept too.
Where are my backups?
In your Documents folder, in UA-101 Backups. You can copy them to another Mac or to iCloud like any file.
Can other accounts on this Mac use the UA-101?
Yes. The driver is installed once for the whole Mac, and every account gets the sound, the MIDI device and UA-101 Control. If two people are logged in at once, both control the same box.
Can I change the sample rate from the Mac?
No. Only the SAMPLE RATE knob on the box changes it, with the box switched off. See Change the sample rate.
Why does the mixer have only channels 1 to 6?
The box is set to 192 kHz. At that rate the UA-101 has 6 channels each way.
Does the app or the driver send anything over the internet?
No. A report for the developer is only saved as a file; you decide whom to give it to.

Save a report for the developer

A report is one text file with the app's record, the driver's logs, the box's settings and the versions. Nothing is sent anywhere: you decide whom to give it to.

  1. Choose Help → Save Report for Developer…
  2. Keep the name it offers, in Documents, UA-101 Reports, and click Save.
  3. Wait 5 to 10 seconds (it reads the Mac's log of the last day). The Finder opens with the report selected.

Save this manual as a PDF

  1. Choose Help → Save Manual as PDF…
  2. Choose where to keep it (Documents is offered) and click Save. The Finder opens with the PDF selected.

The PDF is the same as this help, to read or print away from the app. To open this help again: Help → UA-101 Control Help. You can also type a word into the search field at the top of the Help menu.

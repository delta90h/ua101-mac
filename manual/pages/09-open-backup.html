Go back to a backup (Open Backup)

Open Backup sets the patch bay and the mixer back to how they were when a backup was saved. It changes the settings in use; the five memories in the UA-101 stay as they are.

First: turn the monitors down and take headphones off. Volumes, mutes and the patch bay change at once, and what you hear can jump.
  1. Choose Device → Open Backup… (under Backup files on this Mac).
  2. Choose a backup file (in Documents, UA-101 Backups) and click Choose. Nothing is sent yet.
  3. Read the window: THE BACKUP (its name and when it was saved), WHAT WILL CHANGE (each setting, from → to), and the red Warning: Open Backup?
  4. Click Open Backup to go on, or Cancel.
  5. The Messages window says what it did and whether the box now matches the backup.

Before anything is sent, the app saves a backup of the settings in use by itself, so you can go back again with Open Backup.

If the box already holds everything in the backup, the window says so and nothing is sent.

What can't be put back

The switches on the box: the sample rate, MONO, SOFT CTRL and the digital input. They are switches, so only your hand moves them. The window lists any that differ from the backup.

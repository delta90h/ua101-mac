Change how the app looks (View menu)

Nothing in the View menu changes the UA-101. The appearance, the message text size and the own-window choices are kept for the next time; the window size too, with Remember Window Size. Always on Top is off each time the app opens.

ItemWhat it does
Actual (100%) (⌘0), Medium (80%), Small (60%)Three fixed sizes of the mixer window. A tick marks the one in use.
Zoom In (⌘=), Zoom Out (⌘−)A step larger or smaller.
Show Messages (⌥⌘M)Opens the Messages window.
Larger Message Text (⌥⌘=), Smaller Message Text (⌥⌘−)The size of the text in the Messages window.
Always on TopKeeps the mixer and the Messages window above other apps' windows.
Remember Window SizeOpens the mixer at the size you left it.
Patch Bay in Its Own WindowTicked: Patch Bay opens as a window of its own. Not ticked: as a sheet on the mixer.
Driver Settings in Its Own WindowThe same for Driver Settings.
AppearanceFollow the System, Dark or Light.

You can also drag the window's corner: the mixer grows or shrinks to fit.

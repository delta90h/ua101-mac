Open the app and read the status light

Open the app

  1. Switch the UA-101 on and plug it into the Mac.
  2. Open UA-101 Control. The mixer window opens.
  3. Wait a moment while the app reads the settings. The light at the bottom right says Reading the settings…, then Ready.

You don't have to type anything or switch anything on: when the light says Ready, every change you make goes to the UA-101.

To control the mixer, the SOFT CTRL switch on the box must be pressed in. If it is out, the app tells you so; see Use the mixer.

The status light

The light at the bottom right of the mixer says what the app can do now.

The light saysWhat it meansWhat to do
ReadyThe settings are read. Your changes go to the UA-101.Nothing.
Changes lockedYou chose Device → Lock Changes. Nothing is sent to the box.Device → Lock Changes again to unlock.
No UA-101The box is switched off or not plugged in.Switch it on, check the USB cable.
Reading the settings…The app is reading the box.Wait a moment.
The UA-101 doesn't answerThe box is there but doesn't reply. The app asks again every 3 seconds.Check the cable; switch the box off and on.
The UA-101 kept another valueThe box did not take a change as sent.Read the Messages window.
Look onlyThe driver passes on no changes. You can look, not change.Save a report (Problems and questions).

At 192 kHz the light also says At 192 kHz: channels 1 to 6 only. That is how the UA-101 works at that rate.

The bottom of the window

  • DEVICE: the USB lamp glows red while the UA-101 answers over USB. Dark: no unit.
  • SAMPLE RATE: the rate the box is set to. The knob is on the box; the app only shows it and never changes it.
  • DIGITAL IN: whether the box uses its optical input as its clock (SYNC) or its own clock (clock: internal).
  • DIRECT MONITOR: the box's MONO and SOFT CTRL switches, in or out. They are switches on the box: only your hand moves them.

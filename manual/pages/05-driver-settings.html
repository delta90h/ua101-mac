Set the driver's delay (Driver Settings)

Driver Settings chooses how the driver trades delay against safety from clicks. Nothing here changes the UA-101 itself.

  1. Choose Device → Driver Settings… (or press ⌘,, or click Driver Settings… at the top of the mixer).
  2. Drag a slider to a new value. The sentence under it says what that value needs or does.
  3. Click Apply. If sound is playing, it stops for a moment and carries on.
  4. Click Close.

The settings

SettingShorterLonger
Playback delay
How early the Mac hands sound to the driver.		 Software instruments answer faster. Needs a larger buffer in the music app (shown under each value, for example buffer 128+). More room when the Mac is busy. At 48 kHz, from 10 ms on, any buffer works.
Recording margin
How far behind the box the recording is read.		 Less delay when you listen through the Mac. 1 and 2 ms gave gaps and clicks in the test. Safer against gaps. The music app's buffer doesn't change it.
  • Rate correction: keeps the box's own clock in step with the Mac. Leave it on. Off passes the samples through untouched.
  • Defaults: puts back the values the driver came with (playback 8 ms, recording 8 ms, rate correction on). Click Apply to use them. Playback at 8 ms needs the music app's buffer at 128 frames or more (at 48 kHz). With a smaller buffer, choose 10 ms or more.

When to turn rate correction off. Only when the exact original samples must reach the box, for example:

  • Dolby or DTS sound sent bit for bit to the optical output, for an amplifier to decode.
  • A test that compares what comes out with what went in, sample by sample.

For everything else, leave it on. The app never turns it off by itself.

The buffer size is not set here: each music app has its own (Logic Pro: Settings → Audio → I/O Buffer Size).

Measured

While sound runs, the Measured box shows the least room that was left at the busiest moment, for playback and recording, and whether anything came late or went missing. Green is fine. With less than 1 ms of room, or with sound lost, choose a longer value.

The window can be a sheet on the mixer, or a window of its own: View → Driver Settings in Its Own Window.

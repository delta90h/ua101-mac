Start again from the factory settings (Reset to Factory and Reset Mixer)

First: turn the monitors down and take headphones off. What you hear can change at once.

Reset to Factory

Puts settings back to the way the UA-101 was delivered. You choose what to reset.

  1. Choose Device → Reset to Factory… (under Memories in the UA-101), or click Reset… at the top of the mixer.
  2. Under WHAT TO RESET, choose one:
    • The settings in use (Current Setting); the memories stay
    • One memory; the settings in use stay: then click the memory's number
    • Everything: the settings in use and all five memories (All Settings)
  3. Click Continue…. Nothing is sent yet.
  4. Read the red warning, then confirm with the red button, or click Cancel.

A backup of the settings in use is saved first, by itself. What a memory held is in no backup unless you recalled it earlier. Everything overwrites all five memories: think twice.

Reset Mixer

Puts only the mixer back as delivered: no channel muted, every one at 0 dB, pan, link and solo as delivered. The patch bay and the memories don't change.

  1. Choose Mixer → Reset Mixer…
  2. Read Warning: Reset the mixer?
  3. Click Reset the mixer, or Cancel.

A backup of the settings in use is saved first, by itself; to go back, use Device → Open Backup… with the file … before Reset Mixer.ua101.

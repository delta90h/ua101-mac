Safety habits

Protect your ears and speakers

  • Monitors down, headphones off before a Recall Memory, Open Backup, Copy Backup to Memory, Reset to Factory or Reset Mixer. Volumes, mutes and the patch bay can change at once.
  • Before Hard Thru in the Patch Bay: make sure no cable runs from an output back into an input, or it makes a loud feedback loop.
  • The app never sets a volume above 0 dB.

Protect your settings

  • Save a backup (Device → Save Backup…) when the box sounds the way you like, and before trying something new.
  • Read the red Warning before you confirm. Save Memory and Reset to Factory overwrite a memory, and what a memory held is in no backup unless you recalled it earlier.
  • Use Device → Lock Changes during a recording or a concert, so a stray click changes nothing.
  • When you are done with Pause Auto-Save, choose Resume Auto-Save before switching the box off.

The box's own switches

  • Move the SAMPLE RATE knob only with the box switched off.
  • Keep the HI-SPEED switch on the back up: down gives 2 channels only.
  • Keep SOFT CTRL pressed in while you use the mixer.

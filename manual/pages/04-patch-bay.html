Choose what each output plays (Patch Bay)

The patch bay chooses what each pair of OUTPUT sockets on the box plays: the monitor mix, an input pair straight through, or a pair the Mac plays (WAVE OUT).

Change one output

  1. Choose Device → Patch Bay… (or press ⌘B, or click Patch Bay… at the top of the mixer).
  2. Find the row of the output, for example OUTPUT 3-4. The green light shows what it plays now; the words at the right say it too.
  3. Click a dark point in that row. The light moves there at once, and the change is read back from the box.
  4. Click Done.

What a row can play

  • MAIN, under MIX: the monitor mix, MAIN OUT in the mixer.
  • INPUT 1-2 … 9-10 (straight through): an input pair as it comes in, not through the mixer.
  • WAVE OUT 1-2 … 9-10 (from the computer): a pair of channels the Mac plays.

The two presets

  • Hard Thru…: every INPUT pair straight to the same OUTPUT pair. It asks first: click Send Hard Thru to go on, or Cancel.
    Careful: with Hard Thru, a cable from an output back into an input makes a loud feedback loop. Turn the monitors down first.
  • Initial Setting: every WAVE OUT pair to the same OUTPUT pair, as the box is delivered.

The patch bay works whether SOFT CTRL is in or out.

The window can be a sheet on the mixer, or a window of its own: View → Patch Bay in Its Own Window.

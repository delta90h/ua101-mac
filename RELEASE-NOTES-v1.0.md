UA-101 Driver 1.0, the first finished release.

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later), macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Tested on macOS 27.2 on an M1 MacBook Pro.

- The UA-101 as a sound device for every app, at 44.1 to 192 kHz.
- UA-101 MIDI for every MIDI app.
- UA-101 Control: the direct monitor mixer, the patch bay, the box's five memories, backups on the Mac, Driver Settings.
- Every account on the Mac gets all of it.
- Uninstall from the app's own menu: keep your settings for later, or remove everything.
- The manual in the app's Help menu, and as `UA-101 Control Manual.pdf` here.

Install: double-click `UA-101 Driver.pkg`. It isn't signed through Apple's paid developer program, so the first time macOS won't open it: click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (see the README).
